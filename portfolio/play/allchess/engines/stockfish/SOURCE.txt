Stockfish 18 (lite, single-threaded WebAssembly build)

These two files (stockfish-18-lite-single.js and .wasm) are unmodified copies
from the npm package "stockfish" 18.0.7 by Nathan Rugg, a WebAssembly port of
the Stockfish chess engine. AllChess runs it in a Web Worker and talks to it
over the UCI protocol.
