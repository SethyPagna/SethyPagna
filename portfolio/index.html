        About me

        I’m Pagna (Sethy Pagna Ung, or James), a Computer Science student at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, with graduation expected in July 2027. I build software for everyday work, learning and play, with a particular interest in useful AI tools and in software for Cambodia.

        Some of it is in real use: Business OS runs a cosmetics shop’s tills and storefront in Khmer and English. Some of it you can play right here, from a Sandline match against bots to Living Kingdom’s floating island and valley.

        I grew up in Cambodia and have studied in Malaysia, China, Australia and Hong Kong. Off the keyboard: basketball, football, badminton, swimming and table tennis.
